Did you know?

After 10 hours alone, the brain reacts to pictures of people the way a hungry brain reacts to food.

Scientists scanned volunteers after a day with no contact. The same craving area lit up for faces as it did for food after a fast. Connection is a need, like eating.

Tomova et al., 2020, Nature Neuroscience
