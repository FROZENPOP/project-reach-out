How isolation impacts brain development

Your brain is built to connect.

A teenage brain is still wiring itself, and other people are part of how it does that. Too much time alone changes the wiring. A little connection protects it. Here are six small ways to start.

The 24-hour experiment

For one day, connect on purpose. We measure how you feel before and after, with no names attached, and publish what the whole group found.

1. Check in Answer three questions about connection, mood and stress. One minute.
2. Pick two or three Talk to someone new. Eat with someone. Have a phone-free conversation. Do something with a friend or family member.
3. Check out Come back a day later with your code, answer the same three questions, and see what changed.
