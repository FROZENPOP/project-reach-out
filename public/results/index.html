      How to read this

      • Volunteers chose to take part. People who sign up for a connection challenge may already be different from people who do not.
      • There was no comparison group of people who did not do the challenge.
      • The ratings are self-reported, from 1 to 5, and cover one day.
      • So this shows what happened for this group. It cannot prove that connecting caused the change.
